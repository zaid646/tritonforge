Method

  1. Weights are quantized offline to int4 with per-group (128 along K) asymmetric scales and stored K-major so the kernel loads dequantized tiles without any register transpose.
  2. The fused GEMM splits the packed byte into even- and odd-k nibbles and issues two tensor-core dots per K step; scales/zeros are fp16.
  3. Every kernel is validated against a PyTorch oracle and swept by triton.autotune.
  4. Results are captured on the target GPU by scripts/run_gpu_suite.sh and gated in CI by scripts/check_regression.py.
